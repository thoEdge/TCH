Résumé checklist

Check each point before you send your résumé.

  • Fits on one page and uses a clean, readable font.
  • Contact details, GitHub or LinkedIn links are correct and clickable.
  • Skills are grouped (languages, tools, frameworks) and match the role.
  • Each project says what you built, which technologies you used, and the result.
  • Education, achievements, and certificates are listed with dates.
  • No spelling errors; saved as a PDF with a clear file name.

Interview practice

Practise answering these out loud, in under two minutes each.

  • Tell me about yourself.
  • Walk me through your strongest project. What was hard, and how did you solve it?
  • Why this role and this company?
  • Describe a time you worked in a team and handled a disagreement.
  • Explain a technical concept you know well as if to a beginner.
  • Where do you want to be in three years, and what are you doing to get there?
